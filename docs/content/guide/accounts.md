# Accounts and signing in

## There is no password

GradeBook signs you in with a code sent to your email. Enter your address, check your inbox, type the 8-digit code. That is the whole of it — nothing to choose, nothing to forget, nothing to reset.

The code is short-lived. If it stops working, use **Resend code**; there is a short cooldown between sends, shown on the link. If a code is rejected, the boxes turn red and clear themselves so you can retype without deleting anything first.

Signing in on a phone and a laptop at the same time is fine. You stay signed in until you sign out.

> **Not receiving the code?** Check spam first. If it is genuinely missing, the address may not be the one your school registered — ask an administrator which address is on your record rather than trying other addresses.

## Your first sign-in

New accounts are asked for a first and last name, and for **which kind of account** this is:

- **Staff or faculty** — teacher, administrator or other school staff.
- **Student** — joins a school and sees their own grades.

Choose carefully. This decides which application you land in — staff go to the dashboard, students to the portal — and it is not something you can flip yourself afterwards. If you pick wrong, an administrator has to correct it.

## Staff: joining or creating a school

After your profile, staff are asked about a school.

### Join an existing one

Search for your school and send a **join request**. An administrator there approves it, and until they do, you can sign in but will see very little. This is the normal path for everyone except the first person.

### Create one

The first person at a school creates it: a name and whether it is **primary** or **secondary**. Whoever creates it becomes its administrator.

Only do this if your school genuinely is not listed. Creating a second school for an existing one splits your colleagues across two, and the records do not move between them.

## Students: joining with a school code

Students do not search for a school or request access. They are given a **join code** by the school — three blocks of four characters, like `RXKT-9WMB-2FQH` — and enter it after signing in.

The code:

- is issued by school staff and works for the whole school, not one student;
- expires after a set number of days;
- can be revoked, which stops it immediately.

Enter it in full, including the dashes. Once redeemed, you are in the school and the portal opens.

If it is refused, the likely reasons are that it has expired, been revoked, or been mistyped — codes are shown once and re-typed by hand, so a wrong character is common. Ask whoever gave it to you for the current one.

> **What the code cannot do:** it lets a student in but cannot know *which* student they are. If the school already had a record under your name, joining creates a second one, and staff merge the two — see [Setting up your school](./school-setup.md). Your grades are safe throughout; the merge keeps the existing record and its history.

## Your profile and settings

**Settings** holds your name and profile picture. Your email address is your identity and is not editable here — ask an administrator if it has to change.

The light/dark toggle sits in the top corner and follows your device by default.

## Signing out

Use the menu under your name. On a shared computer, sign out rather than closing the tab: the session survives a closed browser by design.

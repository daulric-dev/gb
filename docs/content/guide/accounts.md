# Accounts and signing in

## There is no password

Type your email address and press **Continue**. What happens next depends on your school:

- **Your school uses single sign-on and requires it** - you go straight to your school's own sign-in page (Google, Microsoft or the school's system) and come back signed in. No code is sent.
- **Your school offers single sign-on** - you see **Continue with Google** (or Microsoft, or your school), and **Email me a code instead** underneath.
- **Otherwise** - GradeBook emails you an 8-digit code. Type it in and you are signed in.

A code lasts ten minutes and works once. Asking for a new one cancels the old one. After five wrong tries the code stops working - ask for a fresh one.

You stay signed in for 30 days on each device, renewed each time you use it. Signing out ends the session on that device only.

> **Not receiving the code?** Check spam first. If your school requires single sign-on, codes are switched off for your school's addresses - use the sign-in button instead.

## Your first sign-in

New accounts are asked for a first and last name and whether you are **staff** or a **student**. If you signed in with your school's account, the name is filled in for you.

Choose carefully: staff go to the dashboard, students to the portal, and it is not something you can switch yourself.

## Joining your school

### Automatically, with a school email address

If your school has registered its email domain (for example `@srmss.edu.gd`), signing in with that address puts you in the school straight away:

- **Students** join immediately and appear on the school roster.
- **Staff** either join immediately with the role the school chose, or send a request an administrator approves - the school decides.

If you already had an account before your school registered its domain, the schools page shows **Your @domain address belongs to <school> - Join**.

### Staff, otherwise

Search for your school and send a **join request**. An administrator approves it. The first person at a new school **creates** it instead and becomes its administrator.

### Students, otherwise

Enter the **school join code** a teacher gives you - three blocks of four characters, like `RXKT-9WMB-2FQH`. It works for the whole school and expires after a set number of days.

Once you are in a school, you join **courses** either because a teacher adds you or by entering a **course join code** (six characters) under **Join a course** in the portal.

## Your profile and settings

**Settings** holds your name and profile picture. Your email address is your identity and cannot be changed here.

## Signing out

Use the menu under your name. On a shared computer, sign out rather than closing the tab - the session survives a closed browser.

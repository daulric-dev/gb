# The student portal

Students get their own view - on the web and in the mobile app - showing one person's record and nothing else.

## Dashboard and courses

Your course cards, each with your current grade, and what is due next across all of them. **School announcements** - news your school posted for everyone - appear at the top of the dashboard (the newest three), and all of them on the **Announcements** page; in the app they are on **Home**. Staff-only notices are never shown to students. **Join a course** takes the six-character code your teacher gives you. **Courses** also lists past (archived) courses, which stay readable.

Inside a course: **Home**, **Modules** (pages, work, files and links in order), **Assignments**, **Grades**, **Announcements**, **Discussions** and **Attendance**.

The mobile app has the same course screen: tap a course on **Home** and switch between **Home** (the course description, upcoming work and recent announcements), **Modules**, **Work**, **Grades**, **Announcements**, **Discussions** and **Attendance** with the chips along the top. In **Modules**, a page opens in the app, work opens its hand-in screen, a link opens in the browser, and a file is downloaded and handed to your phone's viewer (Quick Look / "Open with"), where you can also save or share it. Pull down on any screen to refresh.

## To-do and handing in work

**To-do** lists everything set for you across courses, by due date.

- **Assignments** - attach a file, type an answer, or both. Attaching is not handing in: press **Hand in** when you are ready.
- **Quizzes** - track how many questions you have answered and see which ones still need an answer. Once all are answered, confirm before submitting; the confirmation explains how the submission affects your attempts. The score appears straight away. If retakes are allowed, **Try again** appears after your result. Correct answers are never shown.
- **Lockdown quizzes** - in the app, the quiz is pinned to the screen: on Android confirm the **Pin this app?** prompt; on iPhone/iPad turn on Guided Access (set it up once in **Settings > Accessibility > Guided Access**, then triple-click the side button and tap **Start**). Screenshots and recordings are blocked and reported, and leaving or unpinning the app is shown to your teacher. After submitting, iPhone users triple-click and enter their Guided Access passcode to end it.
- **Extra time or no Lockdown** - if you need more time or use assistive technology that Lockdown gets in the way of, ask your teacher or school. With an accommodation, the quiz shows your own time limit, and a Lockdown quiz starts without fullscreen or pinning.
- **Offline work** - done on paper; your teacher records the mark here.

Once work is marked you see your score (to two decimal places) and your teacher's feedback. If the work has a **rubric**, it is shown up front so you know how it will be marked, and once marked it shows the level your teacher picked for each criterion. In the app all of this is on the work screen: type an answer and/or **Attach a file**, then **Hand in**; quizzes show answer progress and unanswered question numbers, and ask you to confirm before submission. **Try again** appears when retakes are allowed.

## Using the app offline

The app keeps a copy of your courses on your phone so you can keep reading and working without a signal.

- **Reading.** Course pages, module lists, work instructions and due dates, and your work list are saved for your active courses (refreshed in the background when you are online, and whenever you open them). Offline, screens show **Offline - showing saved copy from** a date and time, and refresh on their own when you are back online. Something you have never opened, and has not been saved yet, needs a connection the first time. **Settings > Offline data** shows how much is stored, saves your courses now, or clears the saved copies.
- **Quiz answers.** Start a quiz online; if the signal drops, keep answering. Answers are **Saved offline** on your phone and sent in order when you are back online (**Syncing**, then **Synced**). **Submitting** always needs a connection.
- **Written work.** What you type in an assignment is kept on your phone as a draft until you **Hand in**, which needs a connection.
- **Deadlines.** A draft is never sent after its deadline: if the quiz time ran out or the work closed while you were offline, the work screen says **This was due while you were offline** and keeps your draft on the phone, where you can view it and share or copy it (for example to send to your teacher with a request for an extension). For a timed quiz, the answers that reached the server in time were submitted.
- **Changed somewhere else.** If you also worked on the same quiz or assignment on another device, the app does not overwrite either copy: it shows **Changed on another device** and lets you compare them, keep this phone's draft or use the server copy.
- **Not offline:** file uploads (they go straight to your school's storage), starting a quiz, submitting or handing in, and **Lockdown quizzes**, which need a connection the whole time because the app has to keep telling your teacher you are still in the quiz.
- **Signing out** deletes everything saved on the phone, including drafts that were never sent; the app warns you first.

## Calendar and notifications

**Calendar** shows a month at a time with every piece of work on the day it is due, coloured by course. Pick a day to see what is due, at what time, and whether you have handed it in. **Export .ics** saves the month's due dates for Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar (a one-off copy; export again to pick up changes). In the app, the **Calendar** tab lists the next two months day by day.

When a teacher publishes new work, posts a course announcement or publishes a discussion topic, or someone replies in a discussion you started or posted in, you get a notification (several replies to the same topic are gathered into one): the **bell** in the portal header shows how many are unread, and the app sends a push. Opening a notification marks it read and takes you to the work, or to the course's announcements - in the app too: a new-work push opens the work screen and an announcement push opens the course on its **Announcements** section. **Mark all read** clears the bell. Draft courses never notify, and work that is closed and reopened does not notify you twice.

When your school posts an announcement for everyone, the app sends a push (tapping it opens **Home**). School announcements are not in the bell; they stay on the dashboard and the **Announcements** page.

## Grades

**Grades** shows every course with your percentage and letter, and each course's breakdown by assignment group, with every marked piece of work, its score and your teacher's feedback. Work marked **Excused** does not count towards your grade.

Your grade is your grade *so far* - unmarked work is not counted yet.

## Discussions

A course's **Discussions** tab lists the topics your teachers have started, with how many replies each has and how many are new since you last looked. Open one to read it and reply; press the reply arrow on a post to answer it directly. Replies are written in Markdown, and **Preview** shows how yours will look.

You can edit or delete your own posts, not anyone else's. Topics can also appear in **Modules**, beside the week's work. Some topics ask you to **post before seeing replies** - your classmates' answers appear once you have posted yours. A **locked** topic takes no new replies, and an archived course's discussions are read-only. If your teacher allows it, **New topic** lets you start your own.

Other students see your name and photo on your posts - never your email.

In the app, open a topic from the course's **Discussions** section: **Post a reply** answers the topic, **Reply** under a post answers that post, and your own posts have **Edit** and **Delete**.

## Checking in to class

When your teacher opens attendance:

- the app sends a notification - tap it to check in;
- the web portal shows a banner with a countdown.

Check in by **scanning the QR code** on the board (your phone's camera or the app's **Scan attendance QR**), or by typing the **4-digit code** shown in the room. If your teacher turned on the location check, allow location - you need to be close to the room. Not signed in yet? Scanning still works: sign in on the same phone and you are checked in. (A scan link only works on the phone that scanned it, so sending it to a friend will not check them in.)

Miss the window and you are marked absent; talk to your teacher if that is wrong. Each course's **Attendance** tab shows your record and rate.

## If something looks wrong

- **A mark is missing** - your teacher may not have marked it yet. If the work says handed in, it arrived.
- **You cannot hand something in** - it may be closed, or the course archived.
- **"That code has expired"** - the QR code changes every few seconds; scan the one on screen again.
